# 🖼️ Image roster

44 pieces of concert gear, each "sitting for its portrait" in a different art style. ⭐ marks the
core 33 to generate first. Every style is used once, so each profile teaches a different artist or
movement.

Each profile shows two images:

- **Art portrait** (`images/art/`): AI-generated, in the style listed below.
- **Real reference photo** (`images/photos/`): a small photo of the real gear, from a manufacturer
  press kit or product page. Its source goes in [`CREDITS.md`](CREDITS.md).

Machine-readable version: [`manifest.csv`](manifest.csv).

## ✅ Checking a generated image

Accuracy beats style. Before an art image goes in `images/art/`, hold it next to a real photo and
check:

- [ ] Every item in **Must be accurate** is right (count the pins, rings, and latches)
- [ ] No text, logos, or brand names
- [ ] 20th-century artists are evoked, not copied (no recreation of a specific copyrighted painting)
- [ ] PG: "male/female" is a connector term only
- [ ] Square, 1024×1024, file named `NN-slug.png` to match the manifest

## 🏛️ Museum notes

A museum note means the artist or movement is in that collection, not that the work is on view.
Check the museum's site before sending students.

- **Getty Center** (Brentwood) · **Getty Villa** (Pacific Palisades): ancient Greek and Roman art
- **LACMA** (Mid-Wilshire) · **The Broad** · **MOCA** (both downtown LA)
- **Norton Simon** (Pasadena)
- **Hilbert Museum** (Orange, at Chapman): California Scene painting
- **OCMA** (Costa Mesa, at Segerstrom Center): modern and contemporary California art, including
  Light and Space
- **Bowers Museum** (Santa Ana): no row points here yet; check its current exhibitions

at LACMA (confident, not yet checked). MOCA's Rothko could only be traced to a 1993 exhibition, so
